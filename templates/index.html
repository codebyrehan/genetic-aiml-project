METHODOLOGY

From signal to oversight.

A transparent pipeline connects feature engineering, machine learning and governance-oriented interpretation.

01

Feature engineering

Mismatch count, PAM correctness, exon location, conservation and GC content form a compact synthetic feature space.

02

Prediction

A Random Forest-style risk model produces an educational score, label, confidence and feature-importance signals.

03

Pattern discovery

K-Means groups synthetic profiles to surface patterns that may deserve deeper scientific investigation.

04

Governance synthesis

Ecological and regulatory engines translate model outputs into review prompts rather than automated decisions.

RESPONSIBLE AI

Beyond the prediction.

AI is decision support — never a replacement for scientific, ethical or regulatory human judgment.

01

Ethical considerations

Unintended modification, transparency, privacy, misuse potential and equitable governance belong in every genetic-technology assessment.

02

Ecological consequences

Gene-drive propagation and unintended population-level effects introduce uncertainty requiring monitoring, containment and expert review.

03

Human oversight

AI outputs are educational risk signals. Final decisions require qualified experts, evidence, context and accountable governance.
