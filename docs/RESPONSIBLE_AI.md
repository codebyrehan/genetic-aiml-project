# Responsible AI

## Scope
Genova is an educational AIML prototype for the PBL project **Challenges in Regulating Genetic Information in Mammals**. It explores genetic, ecological, ethical and regulatory considerations using synthetic data.

## Human oversight
Model outputs are decision-support signals only. They must not be interpreted as clinical, ecological, legal, or regulatory decisions.

## Data and privacy
The prototype uses synthetic educational data and does not require patient genetic information. Real genetic datasets would require appropriate governance, consent, security and privacy controls.

## Model limitations
Predictions can contain false positives, false negatives and dataset-driven bias. Model performance on synthetic data does not establish real-world validity.

## Ecological limitations
The ecological score is a transparent educational proxy. It is not a population model and cannot predict ecosystem outcomes.

## Regulatory limitations
The regulatory layer recommends human review based on prototype signals. It does not approve, reject, certify, or determine compliance with any law or regulatory framework.
